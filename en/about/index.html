About the project and privacy

Energietracker is a private, non-profit open source project. No company, no advertising, nothing to buy.

Who is behind it

Energietracker is developed and maintained by Bingerminger on GitHub, in spare time.

The code is under the GNU AGPL v3.0 or later. Anyone can use, check and pass it on.

Contact

Contact goes through GitHub: questions and ideas in the Discussions, bugs as an issue, security issues confidentially through Security.

Support

A coffee through buymeacoffee.com/bingerminger is voluntary. Ideas are decided by benefit for many, closeness to the bill, effort and privacy.

Privacy on this page

  • No cookies, no tracking, no analysis of visits.
  • Nothing from other servers: fonts, pictures and scripts are on this site itself.
  • In your browser the page remembers only two things: your choice of appearance and whether you closed the language note. Both stay on your device; you can delete them in your browser settings.
  • The host is GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). According to GitHub, the IP address is logged for security when a page is visited, even without signing in. Data collection on GitHub Pages, GitHub privacy statement.
  • Links to the outside lead to GitHub, the demo, Home Assistant and buymeacoffee. Only when you click them do their rules apply.
  • The demo runs entirely in your browser, with invented sample households.
  • Responsible for this page is the provider named in the legal notice.

The app itself

What Energietracker talks to from your home is listed in the README under Your data: by default only the weather sync, which can be switched off.
